It has been interesting going back to work and getting up to speed with my new
team's systems, some of which are third-party and others are Very Much Invented
Here. I find myself thinking about this quotation a lot:

> It seems that perfection is attained not when there is nothing more to add,
> but when there is nothing more to remove.

--[Antoine de Saint-Exupéry]

The author of [Le Petit Prince] was an aviator and was referring to the
engineering of the modern aeroplane but he could just as easily have been
talking about computer software. As I wrote in this blog over a decade ago,

> When we write code, particularly code for an already-solved problem, we are
> not really contributing a solution but adding to the problem!

--[Part of the problem, or part of the solution?]

Less is more.
