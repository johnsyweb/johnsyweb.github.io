It's been a while since my last parkrun post. Today I was down the creek at [Kirkdale Reserve parkrun] for my twelfth finish there. That puts me a step closer to lifting my [p-index] to 12, the goal I set out [earlier in the year].

[![My p-index table showing eleven events at twelve or more finishes, updated 15 August 2026][p-index-table]][p-index-script]

[![Chart of my p-index progression over finishes, updated 15 August 2026][p-index-progression]][p-index-progression-script]

Over the last four months I've been chipping away at the other events that sat between me and that next increment, roughly guided by where _DJ_'s cross-country events have been. What remains is [Studley parkrun]: five more finishes there and the index ticks up. Studley has had a few recent [cancellations] while necessary engineering works take place on the beautiful [Kane's Suspension Bridge], so I hope to enjoy their alternative course in the spring.

_DJ_ had the morning off cross-country and cycled himself to [Coburg parkrun] to walk the event. Proud parent.

Cross-country is at Keilor next week, so I am looking forward to returning to [Brimbank parkrun].
